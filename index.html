How we care

The Daybridge difference.

Every detail intentionally designed for people living with memory loss and the families who care for them.

  • Daytime connection. A structured, social setting supported by dementia-trained staff.
  • Clinical support. Onsite nursing, therapy, and early detection built into every day.
  • For caregivers, too. Respite, training, and community for the whole family.

Shaped by direct input from hundreds of family caregivers and dozens of clinicians, and grounded in leading peer-reviewed research.

A day at Daybridge

Preview a typical day at Daybridge.

Every day follows a calm, structured rhythm, built around connection, movement, and joy.

8:00 – 11:00 AM

Morning

Arrivals, health check-ins, and enrichment activities including word games, discussion groups, and skill-based experiences timed for when minds are sharpest.

11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Midday

Movement sessions including tai chi, dancing, and walking conversations, followed by a freshly prepared hot lunch.

1:00 – 5:00 PM

Afternoon

Creative and purposeful activities including art, music, gardening, storytelling, and opportunities to volunteer.
